# Sample 1: Urgency / Account Suspension (Fictional Example)

> This is a fictional, generic mock-up for educational analysis only. It does
> not impersonate any real company. Links shown are placeholder text, not
> real/functional URLs.

## The Email

```
From: Security Team <no-reply@secure-accountverify.net>
To: user@example.com
Subject: URGENT: Your account will be suspended in 24 hours

Dear Customer,

We have detected unusual activity on your account. To avoid permanent
suspension, you must verify your identity immediately by clicking the
link below and confirming your login details.

[Verify My Account Now]  (placeholder — not a real link)

Failure to verify within 24 hours will result in permanent account
closure and loss of all associated data.

Regards,
Account Security Team
```

## Red Flags Identified

| # | Red Flag | Explanation |
|---|----------|-------------|
| 1 | Sender domain `secure-accountverify.net` | Doesn't match any real company's actual domain — a generic scare-domain |
| 2 | "Dear Customer" | Generic greeting instead of your real name — legitimate services usually personalize |
| 3 | 24-hour countdown | Manufactured urgency to prevent careful thinking |
| 4 | "click the link... confirm your login details" | Legitimate companies never ask you to "confirm" a password via email link |
| 5 | Vague sender ("Account Security Team") | No specific company name, employee name, or verifiable contact info |
| 6 | Threat of "permanent closure" | Fear-based pressure tactic |

## Social Engineering Technique
**Urgency + Fear** — the email manufactures a ticking clock and a scary
consequence (permanent account loss) to short-circuit the recipient's
normal skepticism.

## Correct Response
Do not click. Log into the actual service directly (by typing the known
URL yourself, not via the email link) to check account status. Report the
email as phishing.
